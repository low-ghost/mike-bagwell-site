[Tree Light Books](http://www.treelightbooks.com) hired El Aleph Design to design and print [BYE SEA](http://www.treelightbooks.com#/bye-sea/) by Tony Mancus. The cover artwork is a 3 layer silk-screen. The book's interior contains an original and bold layout designed to enhance the poems' content.

[Read a review in Pank Magazine](https://pankmagazine.com/2014/06/19/review-bye-sea-tony-mancus/): “The typography and page layouts throughout are absolutely gorgeous. In all honesty, this is one of the most aesthetically pleasing reading experiences I have had in quite some time.”
