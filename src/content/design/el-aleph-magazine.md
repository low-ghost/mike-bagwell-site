El Aleph Press’ first magazine is a handmade collection of over 50 phenomenal artists and writers. The publication exploits the full possibilities of print and digital media by combining poetry, fiction, artwork and comics in a hand-bound volume with silk-screened covers, full color interior, a sewn chapbook, and gorgeous painted menko card inserts as well as an extensive online showcase of multimedia poems, videos and spoken word projects linked through QR codes.
