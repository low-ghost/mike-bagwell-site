In addition to [Tony Mancus’s BYE SEA](https://www.mikebagwell.me/design/bye-sea), Mike Bagwell and El Aleph Design created the logo for [Tree Light Books](http://www.treelightbooks.com), chapbooks including _Selections from The Murder Book_By Susan Slaviero and _Necessary Objects_by Rachel Levy, and finally a series of print cards for [Ghost Ocean Magazine](http://ghostoceanmagazine.com), a sister project.
